About the lab

Let us be your micro FT-IR department

{{ company.name }} is a materials laboratory specializing in microanalysis of inorganic and organic materials. We provide analysis services to manufacturing facilities, laboratories, consultants, engineers and researchers.

Our modern micro FT-IR system analyzes polymers, coatings, fibers, rubber and some inorganic materials, and identifies unknown organic contaminants as small as 10 microns. Because micro FT-IR reveals molecular structure, pairing it with SEM & EDS gives a more thorough understanding of a material, which helps in failure analysis and even the analysis of geological samples.
